# Portfolio Contract for Shadow Market Desk

Shadow Market Desk is the scalable Growth Engine in Boran's portfolio. It must obey the Business Operating System maintained in `lemurcina/Ekstenzije/BUSINESS_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`.

## Mission
Turn lawful public signals into evidence-backed opportunity candidates and ultimately realized gross profit, while minimizing capital at risk and preserving human trust.

## Mandatory pipeline
SIGNAL -> EVIDENCE -> REALITY CHECK -> BUYER-FIRST VALIDATION -> SCORE -> OFFER CANDIDATE -> OUTCOME -> LEARNING

## Initial focus
Primary vertical: Los Angeles commercial permits / new businesses / local contractors.

Do not open additional verticals just because data is available. Expansion requires either:
- credible positive economics from the active vertical; or
- a documented finding that the active vertical is structurally invalid and should be replaced.

Expansion order after proof:
1. surplus/liquidation asset intelligence with buyer-first validation;
2. procurement/pre-RFP intelligence;
3. real-estate-related intelligence only where licensing/referral constraints are explicitly satisfied.

## Revenue discipline
- Optimize realized gross profit and evidence-backed expected gross profit per agent-hour.
- Never optimize raw lead, email, scraper or idea volume.
- Estimated or pipeline revenue must not be labeled realized.
- Every strategy has a success metric, sample size, stop condition and capital-at-risk limit.
- Weak strategies are paused and their capacity reallocated.

## Capital discipline
- Default first test cost is zero or near-zero.
- No speculative inventory purchases by default.
- Buyer-first evidence precedes any recommendation to deploy capital.
- Irreversible actions, regulated brokerage/referrals and binding financial commitments remain policy-gated.

## Human standard
Any outward-facing candidate message must be factual, context-aware, concise and respectful. Facts and inferences must be distinguished. No fake familiarity, hype, pressure, unsupported claims or robotic token personalization.

## Integration rule
Agents may implement isolated modules in parallel, but the integrator must refuse blind merges where data models, provenance, policies, outcome accounting or state machines conflict. Cross-module behavior is accepted only after integrated tests demonstrate that evidence, policy, capital limits and outcome accounting remain intact.
